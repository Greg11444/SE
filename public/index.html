Frequently asked questions

How do I get a shipping quote?

Complete the quote form below with your origin, destination and cargo details. A freight specialist replies within one business day with a clear price and transit time.

How is the price calculated?

Pricing depends on the chargeable weight (actual or volumetric, whichever is higher), the route, the service level and any customs or insurance extras. Your quote lists every charge up front.

How long does customs clearance take?

With complete documents, most shipments clear within one to two working days. Delays usually come from missing paperwork, which our brokers check before your cargo departs.

Is my cargo insured?

Basic liability applies to every shipment. We recommend full cargo insurance for high-value goods, and we can add it to any quote.

Which items can’t you ship?

We can’t carry illegal goods, weapons, or items banned in the destination country. Dangerous goods, batteries and perishables need special handling, so tell us about them in your quote request.

How do I track my shipment?

Enter your tracking number in the box at the top of this page. You will also receive email updates at every major milestone.
